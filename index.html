About

Every major studio abandoned hand-drawn animation.
We're building the one that didn't.

Bruce Animation Studios exists at the intersection of traditional craft and modern AI. We produce hand-drawn 2D animation using tools we build ourselves that compress production timelines by orders of magnitude without replacing the artist at the centre of the process.

The long-term vision extends beyond traditional media: original IP, AI entities, and technology that brings animated worlds into physical space. But it all starts with the pencil.

Bruce Interp

The AI in-betweening tool built for 2D animators. Draw less, animate more.

Why animators want this

Spend your time on the art, not the mechancials

In-betweening is one of the most time-consuming part of hand-drawn animation. Bruce Interp handles it so you can focus on key poses, timing, and character acting. The parts that actually matter.

Your line quality, preserved

Purpose-built for pencil line art, not repurposed from live-action video tools. Bruce Interp understands hand-drawn strokes and maintains the aesthetic that makes 2D feel alive.

Go from freelancer to one-person studio

The economics of hand-drawn animation have locked independent animators out of serious production for decades. Bruce Interp changes the equation. Produce broadcast-quality work without a team of twenty.

Built by animators, for animators

Developed inside a working animation studio and tested against real production workflows. Not an AI demo, but a production tool.

How it works

You draw the key poses. Bruce Interp generates the in-between frames using AI models trained specifically for 2D animation interpolation. The output preserves your hand-drawn line quality — clean, expressive, and ready for your pipeline.

Traditional production requires 8–16 in-between drawings for every second of animation. Bruce Interp generates them automatically, cutting the most labour-intensive stage of 2D production from weeks to hours.
